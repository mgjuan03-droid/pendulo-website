Péndulo Gear

Wear the club. Not just the brand.

Péndulo Gear is built the same way the club is — no dilution, no shortcuts. Every piece carries the mark of the community training on our floor, and it's yours the same way membership is: earned by showing up, not added to a cart.

The piece you pull on once the session's done — heavyweight fleece, oversized fit, and a chest mark understated enough for the street and the sled lane alike. This is off-the-floor Péndulo, not workout Péndulo.

Available At the club only
02 — T-Shirt

Athletic fit, heavyweight cotton, cut for training and worn well past it. Black or chalk white — same mark, same momentum, your call on the day.

Available At the club only

Low profile. High mileage.

A soft, unstructured six-panel with the mark up front and "Est. 2026" quietly stitched to the side — built to fade in the sun and travel with you long after opening day.
